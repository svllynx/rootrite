People plant what the nursery has, what the neighbours planted, or whatever grows fastest. A tree that thrives in one town can fail in the next, because seven things change between them: soil, elevation, rainfall, temperature, slope, sunlight and what the tree is meant to do. Get one of them wrong and the seedling dies in its first dry season, or lives fifty years cracking a wall it was never meant to stand beside. RootRite exists so that choosing a tree starts with the ground it will stand in.

First, the ground.

Tell us the soil (clay, loam, sand, rock or tidal mud) and how steep the land is.

Then the climate.

Set your elevation and yearly rainfall. We estimate the temperature from the height.

Then the purpose.

Shade, fruit, timber, a windbreak on the coast, or holding a hillside together.

Then, your trees.

Each match comes with how well it fits, why, and how to look after it.

Right tree, right place.

Not every
tree fits

7

People plant what the nursery has, what the neighbors planted, or whatever grows fastest. A tree that thrives in one town can fail in the next, because seven things change between them: soil, elevation, rainfall, temperature, slope, sunlight and what the tree is meant to do. Get one of them wrong and the seedling dies in its first dry season, or lives fifty years cracking a wall it was never meant to stand beside. RootRite exists so that choosing a tree starts with the ground it will stand in.

First, the ground.

Drop a pin on the map. We read the soil (clay, loam, sand or rock) and how steep the land is.

Then the climate.

We look up the elevation and ten years of rainfall at your pin, and estimate the temperature from the height.

Then the purpose.

Shade, fruit, timber, a windbreak on the coast, or holding a hillside together.

Then, your trees.

Each match comes with how well it fits, why, how to look after it, and when to plant.

Inside the app

Drop a pin on your planting site. RootRite reads its elevation, rainfall, soil and slope; you add sunlight, space and what the tree is for.

A free account gives you the top 3 native species with care tips. Premium adds a suitability score for every factor, 10+ species and side-by-side comparison.

Wrong place

Narra roots lift walls and pipes when it is planted closer than 8 m to a house.

Benguet pine weakens in lowland heat, below about 700 m.

Bakauan dies on dry ground. It needs the tide to reach it every day.

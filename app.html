    When to plant

    Seedlings need time to root before any dry spell, so RootRite looks for about three wet months in a row, using ten years of rain records at your pin.

    Where to get seedlings

    Nurseries near your pin, nearest first. Ones that list the tree in stock come first; for the rest, call ahead.
